# Lemoncherry

Small, honest utility apps for Android.

## Sweep: Hidden Camera Scanner
Five physical checks — magnetic field, infrared, lens glint, Wi-Fi devices and
radios — with a coverage report that tells you what was checked, what was
flagged and what this phone could not check. No accounts, no uploads.
