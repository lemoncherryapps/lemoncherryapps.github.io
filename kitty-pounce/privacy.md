# Privacy Policy for Kitty Pounce

**Last updated:** 31 October 2026

## Who we are

Kitty Pounce is a free Android game published by LemonCherry Apps, a channel of LemonCherry Labs. LemonCherry Labs is responsible for this policy and for how the game handles your information. If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to support@lemoncherrylabs.com.

## The short version

- There are no accounts, no sign-in and no LemonCherry servers. We never receive your progress or any personal information.
- Your progress is saved on your device, and in your Android backup if you have backup turned on.
- Two Google services are involved: **Google AdMob**, which shows the ads, and **Google Play**, which processes the optional Remove Ads purchase.
- The game is not aimed at children under 13.

## Data stored on your device

The game keeps one save file in its private storage on your device. It holds:

- your best score, the butterflies you have collected and the number of runs you have played;
- the upgrades you have bought in the shop (Butterfly Magnet, Lucky Wings, Nine Lives, Cat's Eye), the boosts waiting for your next run, the leap trails you own and the one you use, the cats you own and the one you play as;
- your settings (sound, vibration), whether you have finished the tutorial and which one-time hints you have seen;
- whether you own Remove Ads, as a copy of what Google Play reports.

None of this identifies you, and none of it is sent to us. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the save in your device backup so your progress comes back on a new phone; that backup is stored by Google under your Google account and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes the save from the device. You can remove the backup copy in your Google account's backup settings.

## Permissions and why the game needs them

- **Internet** (`INTERNET`) and **network state** (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): to load ads, to show the consent form, and to talk to Google Play about the Remove Ads purchase. The game itself plays offline.
- **Vibration** (`VIBRATE`): short haptic feedback on hops and landings. You can turn it off in the game.
- **Advertising ID** (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, so ads can use your device's advertising ID as described below.
- **Basic phone state** (`READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE`): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The game itself reads no phone data, and this permission does not give access to calls, contacts or your phone number.
- **Google Play billing** (`com.android.vending.BILLING`): to buy and restore the Remove Ads purchase through Google Play.

The game does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or any other sensitive permission.

## Advertising

Kitty Pounce is free and is paid for by ads from **Google AdMob**: a banner on the game over screen, a full-screen ad now and then between runs (never during a run, and never in your first runs), and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch to continue a run or to double the butterflies from a run.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and sends to Google, encrypted in transit:

- your device's **IP address**, which can be used to estimate your general location;
- **interactions with the app and the ads**, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
- **diagnostic information** about the performance of the app and the SDK;
- **device identifiers**: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google uses this data as described in its own policies: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive this data, apart from anonymous, aggregated reports from AdMob about how many ads were shown.

**Your choices:**

- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalised ads and choose purposes. If you refuse, AdMob can still show limited or non-personalised ads, which use the data above only as needed to serve the ad, measure it and prevent fraud.
- **US state privacy laws:** where they apply, the same Google message lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising.
- **Changing your mind:** where these choices apply, the game's info screen (the "i" button on the title screen) has a Privacy Settings button that opens the form again.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android's settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads, or Settings, Google, Ads, depending on your phone). With it deleted, ads are no longer personalised by it.
- **Remove Ads:** buying Remove Ads (below) stops the game from requesting ads, so the ads SDK no longer sends ad requests.

Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience (Google's PG rating or below).

## Remove Ads purchase

Kitty Pounce offers one optional in-app purchase, **Remove Ads**. It is sold and processed by **Google Play** under Google's terms; we never receive your name, card or payment details. The game only stores, on your device, whether you own Remove Ads, and checks this with Google Play when it starts, when it comes back to the screen and when you tap Restore Purchases. You can see and manage your purchases, and ask for refunds, in your Google Play account. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

## Children

Kitty Pounce is not directed at children under 13, and its intended audience on Google Play is 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you have a concern about ad data, contact us and we will help you reach Google, which holds that data.

## What the game does not do

- No accounts, sign-in or social features, and no chat with other players.
- No analytics or tracking of our own, and no LemonCherry servers.
- No collection of your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is controlled by Google; you can manage it in your [Google account](https://myaccount.google.com/) and through the choices above. You can always write to us and we will answer.
