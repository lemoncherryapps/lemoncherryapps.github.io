# Privacy Policy for Hook Dash
**Last updated:** 29 October 2026

## Who we are
Hook Dash ("the game") is a free Android game published by LemonCherry Apps.
Contact: dev.lemoncherry@gmail.com.

## The short version
The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of ours. Your progress
is kept on your device. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which
serves the ads that keep the game free.

## Data stored on your device
The game keeps a small save file in its private storage on your device:
your best score, coin balance, number of runs played, the simian skins you
own and the one you have equipped, whether you finished the tutorial, and
your sound and vibration settings. It contains no personal information.

Android may include this save file in your device's own backup to your
Google account (Android Backup), so your progress can return when you
restore or set up a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your
Google account settings; we never receive it. Uninstalling the game removes
the save file from the device.

## Permissions and why the game needs them
- **Internet and network state**: used only to load and show ads.
- **Vibrate**: short haptic feedback when you catch a vine, crash or set a
  new best. You can turn it off in the game.

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone,
contacts, photos or files.

## Advertising
The game shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and optional
rewarded ads; a rewarded ad is shown only when you choose to watch one, for
example to continue a run). AdMob may collect and use data such as your
device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it,
and information about ad interactions and app performance, to serve and
measure ads and to prevent fraud. This is described in Google's Privacy
Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in how Google uses
information from apps that use its services
(https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks
for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before serving
personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in the game. You can
also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings
(Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## Children
The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital
consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## What the game does not do
It does not create an account, does not require sign-in, does not collect
your name, email or contacts, does not track you across other companies'
apps for our own purposes, and does not sell data.
