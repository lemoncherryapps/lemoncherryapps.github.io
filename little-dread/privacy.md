# Privacy Policy for Little Dread
**Last updated:** 15 November 2026

## Who we are
Little Dread ("the game") is a free Android game published by LemonCherry
Apps, a channel of LemonCherry Labs. LemonCherry Labs is responsible for this
policy. Contact: support@lemoncherrylabs.com.

Little Dread has its own policy because it works differently from our other
games: it has no ads, no purchases and no internet access. The policy for
Hook Dash and Kitty Pounce is at [/games/privacy/](/games/privacy/).

## The short version
The game works fully offline. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers
of ours, and it does not request internet access. Everything it remembers
stays on your device. We do not collect, receive or share any data from it.

## Data stored on your device
The game keeps a few small files in its private storage on your device:

- your progress: which levels are unlocked, the awards you have earned and
  your best times;
- your Stitch Token balance and the keepsakes you own and have equipped;
- your settings, such as sound, music, vibration and reduced effects;
- whether you have seen the opening and which story hints you have met;
- statistics about your most recent round (for example how long it took,
  how often you were spotted or hid, and whether you escaped), used only to
  tune the game on your device;
- counters the game would use to space out ads in a future version.

None of these files contain personal information, and none of them leave
your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Permissions and why the game needs them
- **Vibrate**: short haptic feedback, for example a heartbeat when you are
  being hunted. You can turn it off in the game's settings.

The game does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone,
contacts, photos or files.

## Advertising
This version of the game shows no ads and contains no advertising or
analytics software. If a future version adds advertising (for example
through Google AdMob), this policy will be updated at this address before
that version is released, describing what the ad provider collects and the
choices you have.

## In-app purchases
There are none. Stitch Tokens are earned only by playing and cannot be
bought.

## Children
The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to
children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from
anyone.

## What the game does not do
It does not create an account, does not require sign-in, does not collect
your name, email or contacts, does not track you across other apps or
websites, and does not sell or share data.

## Changes
Material changes to this policy will be posted at this URL with an updated
date.
